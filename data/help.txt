HOW TO PLAY & CONTROLS
----------------------------------------------
W / Up Arrow: Jump Forward
S / Down Arrow: Jump Backward
A / Left Arrow: Jump Left
D / Right Arrow: Jump Right

SYSTEM HOTKEYS
----------------------------------------------
P / ESC: Pause Game
Enter: Confirm / Select Menu
ESC: Return to Previous Menu

GAME SETTINGS & OPTIONS
----------------------------------------------
- Music Volume: Adjust background music level (0-100%)
- SFX Volume: Adjust sound effects level (0-100%)
- Auto Scroll: ON = Camera moves automatically forward
			   OFF = Camera follows player movement

RANKING & SAVE SYSTEM
---------------------------------------------
- High Scores: View Top 5 highest scores ever achieved
- Save Slots: Save/Load your game progress anytime
			 (BE CAREFUL! Dying clears the current save slot)
